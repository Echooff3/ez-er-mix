This installs EZ-er Mix, a preset player for EZmix packs:

  • VST3 plug-in in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • CLAP plug-in in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP
  • Standalone app in /Applications

Use Customize on the Installation Type step to skip any of them.

Requirements: Toontrack EZmix 3 and its preset packs must already be installed. EZ-er Mix reads them where EZmix put them and ships no presets of its own.

EZ-er Mix is an independent project and is not affiliated with Toontrack.
